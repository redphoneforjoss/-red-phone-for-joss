How it works

Send your message through the form. The Red Phone team reviews submissions for privacy and safety. Selected messages may be shared later without usernames or identifying details. Sending a message does not guarantee it will be posted.

Red Phone for JOSS is an unofficial student-run project and is not affiliated with John Oliver Secondary School or the Vancouver School Board.
